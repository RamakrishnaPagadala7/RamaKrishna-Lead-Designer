About Me

I’m a Lead Designer focused on Motion Graphics, Visual Storytelling, and Product Experiences. I create motion-led narratives that make complex products easier to understand, while using my UI/UX and visual design background to keep the experience clear, purposeful, and engaging.

Over my career, I’ve worked across UX, UI, visual design, motion, prototyping, and product delivery. This combination helps me connect storytelling, interaction, and product thinking — from the first idea through to the final experience.

I’m particularly interested in motion graphics, visual storytelling, AI, and emerging digital experiences, and I’m always exploring how motion can make products more intuitive, expressive, and memorable.
